Instant Expert

A five-minute brief so you can talk to a insurance broking prospect like someone who understands their world — not a caller reading a script.

The labour market they live in

Broking runs on experienced people, and they are in short supply. Firms compete for account managers, broker support and claims staff, and training a new local hire to competence takes months. Meanwhile the renewal calendar never stops, so any gap in the support team is felt immediately by clients.

Why the admin load hurts

Every policy carries a trail: renewal preparation, quote comparison collation, certificates of currency, endorsements, insurer follow-up, claims lodgement and updates, invoicing and CRM updates. When brokers do this themselves, they have less time for advice, cross-sell and relationships — the work that actually grows the book.

The pressures shaping their year

Renewal seasons create sharp peaks, compliance obligations (privacy, licensing, disclosure) are strict, and debtors and unclosed CRM tasks quietly erode the business. Clients expect fast turnaround on certificates and claims, and a slow response can lose an account to a competitor.

Where offshore support fits

Licensed advice, placement decisions and client judgement stay local. A dedicated offshore administrator handles the structured work — renewal prep, certificates, policy collation, claims admin, insurer follow-up and CRM hygiene — lifting broker capacity while control over advice and compliance stays in the firm.

Talk like an insider

Know the language: renewals, COCs, endorsements, SOA/SOAF, binders, closings, claims triage, ICA, broking platforms. Know the tools: Insight/CBS, Winbeat, SVU, iBais, Sunrise, BizCover-adjacent systems. Lead with capacity and turnaround; be precise about licensing boundaries.

Objection handling — insurance broking

How to handle any objection. Acknowledge it sincerely, reframe to the business issue, then ask one question that keeps the conversation open. Never argue and never oversell — the goal is a short meeting for the Commercial Manager, not winning the point on the call.
ObjectionResponse angle
“This is licensed advice.”Advice and placement stay with your licensed brokers — always. We support the administration around them: renewals, certificates, claims processing, CRM. Which is heaviest?
“Client data & privacy.”Central in insurance. Brainbox staff work under managed access, confidentiality and clear policies inside your platform — happy to detail the controls.
“Compliance is strict.”Which is why a trained, consistent resource who learns your process helps. You retain the compliance decisions; the processing gets reliable.
“We tried offshore before.”Usually a scoping and supervision issue. Our managed model trains and supervises the role and covers leave — different from a marketplace VA.
“Renewals are seasonal.”That's the strength — a dedicated resource absorbs renewal peaks without hiring and shedding local staff each season.
“Send me details.”Happy to — is it renewals, certificates or claims admin that hurts most? I'll send the right example.
Know when to stop. A clear "no" frees time for better-fit accounts. Close the loop kindly, set a nurture date if there's a future trigger, and respect every opt-out immediately.
