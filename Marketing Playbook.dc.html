Purpose

This Marketing Playbook is the operational manual for the brand and marketing-capability layers of the Revenue Playbook. It documents how Brainbox builds and protects its brand, produces the collateral and systems every sales conversation draws on, and runs the campaigns that feed demand generation.

It sits beneath the Revenue Playbook and alongside its siblings — the Sales Playbook and the Demand Generation Playbook — and governs the Social Media Playbook beneath it. Where demand generation owns the booked meeting and sales owns the close, this document owns everything upstream: positioning, message, collateral, campaigns and the marketing technology stack.

How this document is organisedOne documented section per topic: how marketing earns its place, the pod that runs it, the Brand Hub, the channels, how campaigns are run, sales enablement, the systems and measures, and the AI transformation journey.

How marketing earns its place

Marketing is the first two layers of the revenue relay — Brand custodian and Marketing capability. It exists to make the sellers effective, not to chase inbound lead volume.

Brand custodian

Owns and protects the Brainbox point of view, positioning and voice — the compounding asset carried into every conversation. Never traded for a short-term lead count.

Marketing capability

Turns positioning into collateral, enablement and systems — the tools every sales conversation draws on, and the campaigns that feed demand generation.

The interfaceMarketing hands demand generation current collateral, sharp messaging and a clean HubSpot system with attribution, and hands the brand layer proof and case studies back from delivered work. It is measured on pipeline contribution and enablement readiness, not enquiry counts.

The capability pod

Three roles, all in the Philippines delivery centre, running on AI rather than manual production — a working proof of the Brainbox offshoring model.

Marketing Manager

Directs the pod · senior role in the Revenue Playbook

Owns brand, positioning and the direction of enablement — deciding what the AI systems produce and holding the standard. Runs founder- and sales-led thought leadership; coordinates with the Commercial Manager and the US Agents as an equal. As the senior resource in the Revenue Playbook, sits on the Brainbox Executive Leadership Team.

Marketing Technologist

The pivotal role · built from the SEO specialist

Owns the website and the HubSpot CRM end to end, and owns attribution so the function can prove its contribution. Builds and maintains the AI automation layer, and leads answer-engine optimisation. The whole function is built around this seat.

Content and Brand Producer

One AI-augmented role · built from social + designer

Produces campaign copy, social content and sales collateral under the Manager’s direction. Runs founder- and sales-led LinkedIn as the main organic channel. Handles routine design and video with AI tooling. Governed by the Social Media Playbook.

Brand & positioning

The senior, compounding layer — the Brainbox point of view on offshoring and people performance, and the voice that carries it. Owned by the Marketing Manager and protected through the transformation.

Owner: Marketing Manager (Executive Leadership Team)

Workflow

  1. Maintain the Brand Hub as the single reference — voice, Australian English, tone, and the “Always Be Kind” principle applied to every message.

  5. Capture delivered-client proof as case studies so the positioning stays concrete, not abstract.

Marketing · Function 06

SEO & answer-engine optimisation

Making the Brainbox point of view found — in classic web search and, increasingly, as a cited source in AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI). Owned by the Marketing Technologist.

Owner: Marketing Technologist

Workflow

  1. Enforce an on-page SEO checklist on every web-facing piece: search intent, title/meta, heading structure, internal links, image alt.
  2. Maintain the priority topic and keyword set (below) in Semrush; focus effort where intent and winnability are highest.
  3. Structure content for answer engines: clear question-and-answer framing, definitions, and scannable summaries that are easy to cite.
  4. Run recurring technical audits (Core Web Vitals, crawlability, mobile) with AI-assisted review; fix in the weekly web block.
  5. Track answer-engine visibility — whether Brainbox is cited for its priority questions.

SEO — recommended target topics

A starting set for web search and answer engines, weighted to buyer intent and to the questions SME owners actually ask. Refine in Semrush against difficulty and volume.

Commercial-intent terms (web search)

  • offshore staffing Australia · offshore teams for small business
  • hire offshore admin / bookkeeper / support staff Australia
  • managed offshore teams Philippines
  • outsourcing vs offshoring for SMEs
  • offshore staff cost Australia
  • HR advisory & people performance Perth / WA
  • sector terms: offshore support for accounting / construction / logistics / NDIS

Question framing (answer engines)

  • “How much does an offshore employee cost in Australia?”
  • “Is offshoring legal / compliant for Australian businesses?”
  • “How do I manage an offshore team well?”
  • “Offshoring vs local hire — what’s the real difference?”
  • “What roles can an SME offshore first?”
  • “How do I keep offshore staff engaged and retained?”
Why question framing mattersAnswer engines cite sources that answer a question cleanly. Each priority question gets a clear, kind, well-structured page or article section — the same content that serves classic SEO.

How Brainbox runs campaigns

Campaigns are how marketing and demand generation act as one engine. Each is a focused, month-long, sector-specific push with pre-identified prospects — documented per campaign and scheduled across the year in the FY27 Campaign Planning Overview.

Shape

One sector per campaign, carried for a calendar month. ~15 pre-identified high-value prospects, plus accounts the Associates research and add, worked to ~40–50 per Associate.

Ownership

The Lead Campaign Manager (Abi) coordinates prospect distribution, oversees Associate activity, and prepares the consolidated end-of-campaign report against the nominated metrics.

StageWhat happens
PlanMarketing Manager sets the sector, message theme and the Industry Insight Article; prospects are distributed.
RunAssociates work the Demand Generation Playbook cadence — first-touch with the Insight Article, calls, LinkedIn, follow-up sequence.
ConvertQualified appointments booked for the seller and tagged to the campaign in HubSpot.
ReportConsolidated month-end report on activity, appointments and opportunities against target.
Cross-referenceCampaign execution follows the Demand Generation Playbook; the annual schedule and operating model live in the FY27 Campaign Planning Overview. Marketing produces the Industry Insight Article and collateral for each.

Enablement is the centre of gravity for the function — collateral current, sharp and ready the moment a seller needs it.

30 daysClaude Projects · Design System

The Brainbox Brand Hub anchors all written and social copy, with on-page SEO built into every web-facing piece. The BB and Blue Zoo Design Systems are built and in use. The focus now is creating client design systems, starting with Razor and WE, so the team produces on-brand client work without starting from scratch.

  • Brand Hub: brand voice, Australian English, tone guide, and templates for social (LinkedIn/Instagram), blog and articles. SEO enforced on every web-facing piece: search intent, headings, meta, internal links.
  • BB Design System: typography, palette, spacing and document templates. Built and shared.
  • Blue Zoo Design System: same structure with BZ brand assets. Built and shared.
  • Razor and WE: build a design system per client — brand assets, palette, templates, and channel and SEO context. Shared with the account team.
  • Map the publishing workflow: HubSpot schedules social in advance. Explore publishing articles direct from Claude via the WordPress connector.
  • Decision by day 30: can proposals be produced in Claude Design repeatably? If yes, proposals move to Claude Design and we exit PandaDoc.
MeasureBrand Hub live. Razor and WE design systems built and shared. First client pieces produced and SEO-checked. Proposals in Claude Design vs PandaDoc decided.

AI journey — 60 & 90 days

60 daysAnalysis · Semrush · Time freed

Work smarter, not just more. Semrush and social data used pragmatically: keep effort on what's working, ease off what isn't, so the team moves faster and frees up time — not optimisation for its own sake. The time freed is what lets the team take on more fractional client work.

  • Semrush and social read for what's actually working. Focus the content plan; don't chase rankings for the sake of it.
  • Recurring outputs flowing through the Projects: faster turnaround, less rework.
  • Hours freed tracked across the team. That capacity is the point.
  • Findings shared simply with the team and Sales, no heavy reporting overhead.
  • Adoption holds: every team member using the Projects, not just the lead.
MeasureHours freed per week across the team. Faster turnaround and lower rework vs baseline. Capacity available for fractional client work.
90 daysClient Services · Fractional work

Put the freed time to work. With BB and BZ running fast, set the team up to take on more fractional client work across design, social, SEO and web. Each client gets a dedicated structure so the team delivers compliant work across disciplines without starting from scratch. Can run as a separate client-services build.

  • Client-services structure per fractional client: brand, channels, SEO and social context, templates.
  • Spans the services delivered: design, social, SEO optimisation, web copy.
  • Shared with the account team. More client work absorbed without more headcount.
  • Light brand and consistency check across BB, BZ and client work.
  • Marketing presents capacity gained and client work taken on at showcase.
MeasureFractional client work taken on with the existing team. Client structures live. Turnaround and rework across accounts vs baseline.
