The operating model
One Lead Campaign Manager gives the year consistency; three Associates give each campaign reach. The 15 pre-identified
prospects per campaign are distributed by the Lead Campaign Manager, and every Associate adds their own researched targets
on top. Each Associate is a Lead Generation Specialist working to the standard, weekly rhythm and targets defined in the Lead
Generation Playbook.
LEAD CAMPAIGN MANAGER — ABI AF
Leads all ten campaigns end to end. Coordinates the
distribution of pre-identified prospects, oversees Associate
activity and quality, and prepares the consolidated end-of-
campaign report against the nominated success metrics. Also
carries an Associate share of the outreach.
ASSOCIATES RF AF DT
Rory, Abi and Dodellysa are each a Lead Generation Specialist
and work every campaign. Each takes an allocated share of the
pre-identified prospects, researches and adds more, works the
Playbook's weekly rhythm (Mon–Fri) across the four weeks of the
month, books qualified appointments for the BDM, and logs all
activity in HubSpot for the consolidated report.

Success metrics & consolidated reporting
Every campaign is measured on the same nominated metrics. The Lead Campaign Manager consolidates all three Associates'
results into one end-of-campaign report. The final measure is leads that become commercial transactions.
BASELINE OUTCOME
The team delivers at least 6 new in-person appointments for the BDM every week (≈ 24+ per campaign month) — the floor below which
the week is not acceptable. In-person is the premium, baselined meeting; video / phone is a fallback only.
