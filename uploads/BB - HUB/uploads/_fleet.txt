1.1 T H E O P E R A T I N G M A N U A L
Purpose of this manual
This manual exists to provide certainty. It removes ambiguity from daily operations, defines how work
flows through the Fleet & Facilities Department, and establishes who does what, when, and why.
Facilities work often fails when it relies on memory, goodwill, or constant verbal instruction. This manual replaces
those habits with structure. It creates a rhythm that is predictable, auditable and repeatable — so good outcomes
don’t depend on any one person remembering the right thing at the right moment.
W H O I T A P P L I E S T O
All Fleet & Facilities staff — during normal operations,
peak demand and weekends, and regardless
of individual availability, workload pressure or
competing priorities.
W H E N Y O U M AY D E V I AT E
Only where safety, legal compliance or guest welfare
requires it. Every deviation is recorded and reviewed —
never made quietly.

1.2 T H E O P E R A T I N G M A N U A L
Assets & functions we manage
The department operates across four environments. Each has a different demand profile and a different tolerance for
delay — but all are managed through the same functions and the same systems.
BBT & BBN commercial buildings
Two buildings, both operating 24×7. Shared spaces are
heavily used at lunch and shift changes, and small lapses
are noticed quickly. Work here is preventative — the
goal is to avoid complaints, not respond to them. Only
BBT has parking, and the company vehicle fleet is
stored there.
Residential & short-stay
Ten residential properties, five run as short-stay
accommodation. Guest expectations are high and
tolerance for defects is low. Work here is time-bound:
check-in readiness is not flexible and cleaning windows
are fixed.
Vehicle fleet
Vehicles support staff transport and operations, and their
condition reflects organisational discipline. Stored and
managed at BBT, with Facilities responsible for
protection, presentation, availability and safety. Repairs
are escalated, not improvised.
One standard, everywhere
However different the environments feel, they share one
safety standard, one set of systems, and one chain of
accountability through the Facilities Manager.

Operating philosophy & escalation
A few principles sit underneath every decision. They explain why the rhythms in the rest of this manual are shaped the
way they are.
Readiness first
Short-stay readiness takes priority
over all other work. Fixed time
windows are protected. The daily
plan is locked once — replanning is
the exception, not the norm.
Use the hours well
Early hours reduce risk before
buildings get busy. Later
hours manage outcomes.
Noise is avoided; visibility is
maintained throughout.
Log once, track to close
Problems are logged once, tracked
until closed, and reviewed for
patterns. Reporting exists to drive
change — never produced for its
own sake.
How a problem moves
Escalation is deliberate, not a reflex. Everyone can see a problem; the question is always the same — resolve, stabilise,
or escalate.
Observed
Anyone notices an issue during cleaning, transport, inspection or daily work — and reports it to Jho rather than fixing
it quietly.
Logged in Asana
Jho captures it as a task with a clear description and photos, so it is visible and traceable from the first moment.
Triaged by May
May assesses urgency against guest readiness, safety and business continuity, then chooses the path:

Stabilise

Closed on confirmation
The task is closed only when completion is confirmed — never assumed. Critical safety items are confirmed personally.
Reviewed for patterns
Recurring faults are surfaced in weekly and monthly reviews so the root cause is addressed, not just the symptom.

Accommodation cleaning framework
Accommodation cleaning follows strict rules that prevent scope creep and overload. With two cleaners, properties can
be turned around in parallel — but only the properties the rules authorise, and only within the fixed window.
The cleaning cycle
Every clean follows the same path, driven entirely by Uplisting. Judgement does not add properties to the list.
Cleaning triggers
No other cleaning is permitted unless guest-paid and
approved by May. This keeps capacity aligned with demand.
The fixed window
All accommodation cleaning happens strictly between
11:00am and 1:40pm. Travel time is planned and
protected. The window is not compressed or extended —
attempts to do so create downstream failure.
Laundry & linen
Laundry volume varies with occupancy and is managed as required. Linen is handled by property, washed, dried,
folded and stored to a consistent standard. Damaged or stained items are removed from circulation, and shortages are
reported promptly. Quality is maintained; improvisation is avoided.

Early operations & vehicle readiness
Arvin and Jong commence. Jong uncovers and safety-checks the fleet at BBT; Arvin runs early inspections across
BBT and BBN. Focus is limited to safety, access and urgent defects. Anything unresolved is escalated to May

Morning staff transport

Pre-start control
On-site presence is established at the BBT concierge desk. Jho reviews Uplisting — arrivals, stay lengths and

Manager triage & lock-in
May commences and works through the summary with Jho. Decisions are made once: which properties clean,
whether a check-in needs attendance, cleaner timing, May’s 2–6 availability, and priority maintenance. Asana

Stand-up
A short stand-up for confirmation, not debate. Each person confirms understanding; no new work is introduced.
Shared clarity for the day.
Accommodation cleaning
Venus and Ivy depart BBT and clean only authorised properties, in parallel, returning by 1:40. Jho monitors guest
messages and escalates readiness risks only. The concierge desk stays covered by whichever of Arvin or Jong is
on site.
Building cleaning — BBT & BBN
The cleaning team restores Levels 2–5 across both buildings, clearing lunch-period residue. This protects two
24×7 environments where hygiene and presentation are highly visible.
Check-in control window
Focus shifts to arrivals. May attends residential check-ins and check-outs and handles guest liaison for visiting
Brainbox clients. Jho manages guest messaging. Arvin and Jong hold the forecourt and concierge. Internal
meetings are avoided on check-in days.
Clean close
Jho prepares the daily report; May reviews exceptions only. Open issues roll forward visibly in Asana. The day
closes without loose ends.

Non-negotiable rules
Everything above reduces to a short list that does not bend. When in doubt, these win.
1 	Uplisting governs accommodation cleaning — the only booking source.

7 	Vehicle licensing and maintenance never lapse; unsafe vehicles are not operated.
8 	Weekend work is exception-based; the monthly safety inspection is mandatory.
