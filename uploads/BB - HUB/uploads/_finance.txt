1 . 1 · T H E O P E R A T I N G M A N U A L
Purpose of this playbook
This playbook provides a clear, transparent and consistent framework for the financial management of the
business — one that complies with statutory obligations and the requisite Philippine Accounting Standards,
and removes ambiguity from how finance work is done.
Finance work fails when it relies on memory or on any one person holding the whole process. This playbook replaces that
with structure: defined systems, separated duties, bounded authorities and a predictable calendar — so the numbers are
accurate, the controls hold, and nothing compliance-critical is ever a surprise.
W H O I T A P P L I E S T O
The whole finance function — the General Manager – Corporate, the Accountant and the Accounting Assistant — and
anyone who logs into the financial systems or exercises a financial delegation.
W H E N Y O U M A Y D E V I A T E
Only where a Director authorises it, or where statutory compliance requires it. Controls and separation of duties are
never bypassed for convenience; every exception is documented and reviewed.
A C O N T R O L L E D D O C U M E N T
T H E A I M
Make finance predictable, auditable and trusted — so good financial outcomes
don’t depend on any one person remembering the right thing at the right
moment.

Financial controls & separation of duties
Separation of duties is the principle that no single person controls an end-to-end process. It gives the function a system of
checks and balances — process integrity with proper security and quality control.
Operation vs record
Separate operational responsibility
from record-keeping responsibility —
the person who acts is not the only
person who records.
Admin vs audit
Separate system administration from
system auditing, and system
ownership from certification — so
review stays independent.
Prepare vs approve
The person who prepares a payment is
not the person who approves or
authorises it. Authorisation follows the
delegations (1.5).

1 . 5 · T H E O P E R A T I N G M A N U A L
Delegations & authorities
The Board authorises others to exercise defined powers within limits. Authority is vested in a position, not a person, and
the Board remains ultimately accountable. The authoritative source is the Schedule of Management Authorities; the key
Finance lines are summarised in 4.2.
1 Exercise authority in line with the law, company policy and any conditions on the delegation — for a proper business
purpose, with no conflict of interest.
2 Never approve a transaction you personally benefit from, and never approve beyond your delegated limit — doing so
breaches the policy and may constitute misconduct.
3 A procurement authority must hold a financial delegation that meets or exceeds the contract’s aggregate annual value
(base price, recurring fees, usage, likely renewals, variations and taxes other than recoverable VAT).

1 . 6 · T H E O P E R A T I N G M A N U A L
Confidentiality & information security
Finance handles the most sensitive information in the business — payroll, bank access, director and commercial data.
Protecting it is foundational, not optional. Read this section as binding.
T H E A B S O L U T E R U L E
Payroll, banking and all personal data relating to staff and the directors is highly
confidential and is not shared with any other party — including those who claim
a “right to know”.
Credentials & access
Strong, unique passwords and MFA on Xero, Wise,
Metrobank and email. Credentials are never shared; banking
authorisation stays with the authorised person only.
Screen & document handling
Lock screens when away; keep payroll and bank data off
personal devices; store records only in approved locations
(Xero, OneDrive). Clean desk; no sensitive print-outs left out.
Fraud & payment safety
Verify any change to supplier or staff bank details
independently before paying. Be alert to invoice and email
fraud; when a request feels off, pause and confirm through a
known channel.
S E R I O U S M I S C O N D U C T
Unauthorised disclosure of confidential financial or
personal information will be treated as Serious
Misconduct. If unsure whether something may be shared,
say nothing and ask a Director first.

Operating rhythm
Finance runs to fixed cycles. Pay runs are fortnightly; statutory remittances and VAT are monthly; budgets, tax and audit fall
across the quarter and year. The calendar is the spine (1.9).
Daily 	Process bills & receipts into Xero, reconcile bank items, monitor cash, action AP/AR as they arise.
Fortnightly 	Payroll run (Wednesdays). Reimbursement claims drafted Monday of pay-run week; paid on pay date.
Monthly 	Month-end close & End of Month report; bank reconciliation; recurring payments; SSS, PhilHealth, HDMF,
HMO, withholding tax and VAT remittances.
Quarterly 	Quarterly VAT and income tax; bi-monthly operating-budget updates and forecasts; HMO & aircon
maintenance cycles.
Annual 	Annual income tax & audited financial statements, annual budget, business-permit & BIR renewals, GIS,
insurance renewals, and the five scheduled audits.
P A Y R U N
Fortnightly, on Wednesdays. Claims arrive in a single email
at least three days before; payslips issue via Xero and pay
releases via Wise.

4 . 4 · R E F E R E N C E
Significant accounting policies
A plain-language summary of the policies that govern the books. The authoritative wording lives in the Finance Operations Manual;
these are the working essentials.
Basis of preparation
General-purpose statements under Philippine Accounting
Standards, on the accrual basis and historical cost — modified
where applicable by fair value.
VAT
Revenue, expenses and assets are net of VAT except where not
recoverable from the BIR. The net VAT due to/from the BIR sits in
payables/receivables.
Fixed assets & capitalisation
Carried at cost (or fair value) less depreciation. Equipment
under the capitalisation threshold (~$1,000) is expensed and
held on an asset inventory listing instead.
Depreciation
Straight-line over useful life from the date ready for use; rates,
residuals and useful lives reviewed each reporting period.
Receivables & payables
Receivables reviewed for collectability; uncollectible debts
written off when identified. Trade payables are unsecured and
normally paid within 30 days.
Employee benefits & leases
Benefits provided for as services are rendered. Finance leases
capitalised; operating-lease payments expensed over the term.
Current vs non-current is judged on the 12-month operating cycle. Figures are rounded to the nearest dollar; the chart of accounts
changes only with the GM’s approval.
