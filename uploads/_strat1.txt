1.1 Overview of the Plan
Brainbox, a business process outsourcing (BPO) company based
in the Philippines, is poised to embark on an ambitious four-year
growth strategy. With a firm foundation of 58 employees—41
billable and 17 non-billable—the company is positioned to expand
its reach, diversify its services, and create new revenue streams.
The plan centres on achieving strategic objectives designed to
strengthen Brainbox’s position in the Australian market, expand
into the United States, and foster sustainable, scalable growth.
Two interim strategic objectives underscore the urgency and
precision of this plan. The first is to rapidly increase the number
of billable staff to 100, ensuring that Brainbox maximises its
revenue-generating potential. The second involves leveraging the
company’s new office building to optimise operational costs by
subleasing space to complementary businesses. This approach not
only offsets rent costs but also cultivates partnerships that could
lead to acquisition opportunities, further reinforcing Brainbox’s
long-term growth and profitability.
With a vision to redefine offshore excellence through innovation
and tailored solutions, Brainbox’s strategic plan aligns operational
goals with its core value of “Always Be Kind,” fostering a culture of
collaboration, efficiency, and growth.

2.1 Background and Current State
Brainbox is a Philippine-based Business Process Outsourcing (BPO) company that has established itself as a trusted partner
for Australian businesses. With 58 employees—41 billable and 17 non-billable—Brainbox offers a diverse range of offshore
personnel across various industries, from academic assessors and engineers to medical professionals and traditional
back-office support roles. While it does not offer call centre services, the company differentiates itself through its ability
to provide specialised and tailored solutions that align with the unique needs of its clients.
Since its inception, Brainbox has focused on delivering exceptional value to its clients, all of whom are currently based in
Australia, primarily Western Australia. This geographic concentration has enabled the company to build strong relationships
and establish a reputation for reliability and quality. However, it also represents an area of risk, as the company is heavily
reliant on a single market. Recognising this, Brainbox has crafted a strategic plan that prioritises diversification, both in terms
of client geography and service offerings.

influences every aspect of Brainbox’s culture, from its internal management practices to client interactions, fostering a
collaborative and respectful environment that drives success.

specialised and back-office functions. Brainbox’s ability
