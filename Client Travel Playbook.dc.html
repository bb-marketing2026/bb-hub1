Section 1 · Orientation

Purpose & how this playbook fits

This playbook defines how Brainbox arranges and supports client visits to the Philippines. It is the internal counterpart to the client-facing Client Travel Guide: the Guide tells the client what to expect; this playbook tells our teams how to deliver it, reliably, every time. It is a subsidiary of the Fleet & Facilities Playbook, and works alongside the Accommodation Playbook for the client’s stay.

Client visits matter because they build the relationship no video call can. Face-to-face time bridges cultural and language gaps, builds trust and confidence in the offshore team, and lets clients see the quality of their people first-hand. A visit that runs smoothly reinforces that trust; a visit that stumbles undermines it. Efficient, warm logistics are therefore a commercial asset, not an overhead.

Who this is forThe General Manager — Service Delivery (who owns the visit and its itinerary), Facilities (transfers, accommodation, transport), IT (access and connectivity), People & Culture and the visited team. One visit, several teams — coordinated to look effortless to the client.
The standard we hold toEvery client is met by name at the airport, has a ready room at LaGrande, a working desk and access on day one, and a single, responsive point of contact throughout. Nothing about their trip should require them to chase us.

Section 4 · Planning

Pre-visit planning & the itinerary

The itinerary is the backbone of the visit. It is built early, with the client, and shared with everyone who has a part to play.

Collect the plan early

The client is asked to share their plans with the General Manager — Service Delivery weeks before arrival — especially weekend plans and any team meetings to be booked. Early notice is what makes transfers, rooms and the leisure program fall into place.

Build & confirm the itinerary

The Facilities Manager drafts a day-by-day itinerary: flights, transfers, meetings, meals, leisure and free time. It is confirmed with the client and circulated to Facilities, IT and the visited team.

Book the logistics

Accommodation held at LaGrande (see the Accommodation Playbook), airport transfers scheduled, meeting room reserved, and any hire car or activities arranged — each with an owner and a confirmation.

Brief the team

The visited team knows who is coming, when, and what the meeting agenda is, so the client’s time is well spent from the first morning.

Client holds come firstA confirmed client visit places an accommodation hold at LaGrande before those nights are released to the short-stay market — the room is never at risk of being sold from under a client.

Section 5 · Entry

Travel & entry requirements — what we brief

We proactively brief every client on entry requirements so nothing delays them at the border. These are the points the General Manager — Service Delivery confirms before travel.

RequirementWhat we confirm with the client
Passport validityAt least six months’ remaining validity for admission to the Philippines.
VisaNot required for short business or tourism trips.
eTravel QR codeRegistered at etravel.gov.ph within 72 hours before arrival; QR presented at boarding. Accommodation listed as LaGrande Residences, Sarmiento Street, Malabanias.
RouteTypically transiting Singapore from Australia or the USA, arriving at Clark International Airport.
MedicationBring prescriptions; confirm any prescribed medicine is permitted in the Philippines.
Power & packing220V; most sockets take AU and US plugs, but advise a travel adapter. Business-casual, modest attire for a conservative, humid climate.
Why we brief, not assumeA client turned back at the border, or stuck without their eTravel QR, is a failure of our planning — not theirs. The General Manager — Service Delivery confirms each point rather than trusting it to chance.

Section 6 · Transport

Airport transfers & local transport

Transport is where a visit is won or lost in the first hour. The airport meet is complimentary, personal and never left to chance.

Airport meet & transfer

Complimentary. After immigration, baggage and customs, the client looks for a Brainbox staff member holding a sign with their name. The driver transfers them to LaGrande. Flight details are confirmed in advance so the meet is on time.

During the stay

Daily transport between LaGrande and the office is arranged around the itinerary. The driver’s schedule is set the day before and protected.

OptionHow we handle it
Hire carArranged on request. We advise that Filipinos drive on the right (opposite to Australia) and traffic is busy — offered with a clear caution.
Trikes (local)A trike station sits outside LaGrande; the norm for local trips (~200 PHP / ~$5 AUD across the metro). We brief clients who prefer to travel like a local.
Brainbox transportThe default for office and itinerary travel — comfortable, reliable and included.

Section 7 · Stay

Accommodation

Visiting clients are accommodated, complimentary, at LaGrande Residences. The stay itself is run under the Accommodation Playbook; this page covers the client-visit specifics.

What we provide

Complimentary accommodation at LaGrande for the duration of the visit — a premium, secure residence with 24/7 security, concierge, pool and wellness facilities. The unit is prepared to the same turnover standard as any guest stay.

Readiness

The room is confirmed ready before arrival: cleaned, checked, safe, with access working. Facilities blocks the nights in Uplisting as a client hold so the room is guaranteed.

Welcome & on-site support

A welcome is prepared for arrival. Any issue during the stay routes to Facilities through the single point of contact and is handled fast, as for any guest.

Section 9 · Experience

Leisure program

Beyond team time, a curated leisure program leaves clients with a genuine sense of the Philippines — and lasting goodwill toward Brainbox.

What we can arrange

A program tailored to the client’s interests: outdoor adventures such as wave boarding, shopping at modern malls, local and international dining, or relaxation at the residence’s wellness clinic. Weekend excursions to beaches and tourist destinations where time allows.

How we coordinate it

The Facilities Manager confirms interests during planning and builds leisure into the itinerary. People & Culture and the team help host. Activities, transport and any bookings are arranged and confirmed ahead of time.

Purposeful, not fillerThe leisure program exists to deepen the relationship and give the client a real feel for local life — it is planned with the same care as the working agenda, and sized to the visit and the client’s energy.

Section 14 · The rules

Non-negotiable rules & the outcome we want

  1. One owner. The General Manager — Service Delivery owns the visit and is the client’s single point of contact throughout.
  2. Plan early. Itinerary and logistics are set weeks ahead, not improvised on arrival.
  3. Meet every client by name. No client arrives to an empty arrivals hall.
  4. Room ready, access ready. Accommodation and office access are confirmed before the client lands.
  5. Complimentary is complimentary. Accommodation and transfers are provided; scope of extras agreed in planning.
  6. Safety first, always. Client wellbeing overrides every other consideration.
The outcome we wantA client who leaves with valuable team time, real trust in their offshore team, and lasting memories of the Philippines — having never once had to chase us for anything. Delivered with care and, in every interaction, kindness.
