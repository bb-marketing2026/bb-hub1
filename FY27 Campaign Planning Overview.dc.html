The operating model

One Lead Campaign Manager gives the year consistency; three Associates give each campaign reach. The 15 pre-identified prospects per campaign are distributed by the Lead Campaign Manager, and every Associate adds their own researched targets on top. Each Associate is a Demand Generation Specialist working to the standard, weekly rhythm and targets defined in the Demand Generation Playbook.

Lead Campaign Manager — Abi AF

Leads all ten campaigns end to end. Coordinates the distribution of pre-identified prospects, oversees Associate activity and quality, and prepares the consolidated end-of-campaign report against the nominated success metrics. Also carries an Associate share of the outreach.

Associates RF AF DT

Rory, Abi and Dodellysa are each a Demand Generation Specialist and work every campaign. Each takes an allocated share of the pre-identified prospects, researches and adds more, works the Playbook's weekly rhythm (Mon–Fri) across the four weeks of the month, books qualified appointments for the Commercial Manager, and logs all activity in HubSpot for the consolidated report.

Leading the team — Abi's cadence

Abi's supervision rhythm turns oversight into coaching — keeping each campaign on track while helping Rory, Abi and Dodellysa grow toward their full potential. It runs alongside the Playbook's weekly rhythm, not on top of it.

Weekly · 30 minTeam review of activity vs target and the in-person baseline, then a short 1:1 with each Associate.Accountability & support
Weekly · 20 minQuality review — listen to one call and read two message threads per Associate; coach on a single thing.Skill lift

Coaching focus in every 1:1

  • Celebrate a win, then name one thing to improve
  • Review real calls & messages, not just numbers
  • Unblock data, tooling or targeting issues
  • Agree one specific action before next week

Helping each Associate reach potential

  • Skills matrix: research · outreach · calling · qualifying · booking
  • One stretch goal per month, tracked openly
  • Pair strengths — buddy up on tougher sectors
  • Recognise the behaviour, not only the result
Always Be Kind — in management too. Feedback is specific, timely and kind; targets are firm but support comes first. The aim is a confident team that clears the weekly in-person baseline and keeps getting better.

Success metrics & consolidated reporting

Every campaign is measured on the same nominated metrics. The Lead Campaign Manager consolidates all three Associates' results into one end-of-campaign report. The final measure is leads that become commercial transactions.

Baseline outcomeThe team delivers at least 6 new in-person appointments for the Commercial Manager every week (≈ 24+ per campaign month) — the floor below which the week is not acceptable. In-person is the premium, baselined meeting; video / phone is a fallback only.

Nominated success metrics

  • Qualified appointments booked & held
  • Appointments → sales opportunities
  • Opportunities → proposals
  • Proposals → commercial transactions (final measure)
  • Sales acceptance of leads · no-show rate · data quality

End-of-campaign report (Abi)

  • Consolidated activity & outcomes vs target
  • Per-Associate contribution (RF · AF · DT)
  • Outcome funnel through to commercial transaction
  • Best angles, triggers & top objections
  • Data quality & recommendations for the next campaign
Final success measureAcross the year, the number of leads that turn into sales opportunities and subsequently become a commercial transaction. Every campaign metric is a leading indicator of that single revenue outcome.
