Getting the team ready for 01 August

A day-by-day, hour-by-hour plan to build the Demand Generation team’s capacity before the playbook comes into effect — four working weeks from Monday 6 July, balanced across role competencies, Brainbox familiarity and offshoring familiarity, with Abi leading and two new Associates ramping to independent delivery.

BRAINBOX

Purpose

The Demand Generation Playbook comes into effect on Friday 1 August 2026 — with the first full operating week beginning Monday 4 August. This plan gets the team ready to deliver from that first week, rather than learning on the job once the clock is running.

The starting point is uneven. Abi is a senior, experienced Associate who already knows the motions, the tools and the market — she leads the ramp and carries delivery. The two other Associates are brand new and start from first principles. The plan runs two tracks in parallel: Abi sets up the function and coaches, while the new Associates move deliberately from reading the playbook, to practising, to supervised live work, to independent delivery.

Every day follows the standard working shape — 8:30 to 5:00, Monday to Friday, with an hour for lunch and fifteen-minute breaks mid-morning and mid-afternoon. Time is balanced across three streams: role competencies (80%), Brainbox familiarity (10%) and offshoring familiarity (10%), with the two familiarity streams front-loaded into the first week.

How this plan is organisedA readiness overview, roles and the working-day shape, then each week laid out in detail across two landscape pages — every block built out with what it covers and its output — closing with a go/no-go readiness checklist for the Corporate Services Director.

Roles during the ramp

One experienced lead, two new starters, and a clear oversight line. The ramp is deliberately front-loaded onto Abi so the new Associates never practise a bad habit unobserved.

Abi — Lead Associate

Sets up · trains · supervises · carries delivery

Provisions systems, runs all training, models every motion, reviews the new Associates’ work before it goes live, and keeps the Australian pipeline warm herself throughout — so the function is already moving when the new Associates join it. Owns the readiness call with the CSD.

The two new Associates

Brand new · onboarding to independent delivery

Start from first principles. Learn the company and the offshoring model, then the ICP, personas and tools; practise research, messaging and calls; then move to supervised live work. By week 4 each runs the full weekly rhythm under light supervision and is signed off to deliver.

OversightThe Corporate Services Director holds a short check-in at the end of weeks 1 and 3, and chairs the go/no-go readiness review at the end of week 4. Abi reports progress against this plan at each.

Go-live & first week

The Demand Generation Playbook takes effect on Friday 1 August; the first full operating week begins Monday 4 August. From that week the standards in the playbook apply in full — the weekly rhythm, the lead scoring and compliance, and the baseline of at least six in-person appointments for the Business Development Manager each week, measured as booked and attended.

Abi continues to carry Australian delivery and holds the standard across the team; the two Associates join the baseline at the level the go/no-go review confirmed, growing into full independent delivery over the following weeks. The first monthly outcomes report at the end of August is the first true read on the team’s combined capacity.

The point of the rampFour weeks of deliberate preparation means the team spends August delivering, not learning — and does it kindly, to standard, from the first week.
