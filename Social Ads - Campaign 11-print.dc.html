Most firms do not need a portfolio-wide offshoring strategy. They need one role, in one company, that works.

That is how nearly every Brainbox relationship starts. We scope the position, recruit to your brief, train and supervise the person, and cover them when they are away. Measure it for a quarter. If it works, repeat it in the next company.

It is a managed model, not a freelance marketplace — usually the difference between offshoring that sticks and offshoring that quietly fails.
