Why this industry

The pressure

Mobilisation, procurement follow-up, HSEQ document control, maintenance administration, tender support and reporting absorb the time of managers who need to stay focused on delivery. These suppliers face major-client standards without the internal shared-service structure of a major.

The opportunity

The strongest use case is structured operational support: document control, procurement admin, mobilisation tracking, timesheet processing, maintenance-planning support, reporting and CRM discipline — supporting the people who keep the work moving, not replacing field capability.

Market shapeAvoid the mining majors. Target the suppliers to mining — services, maintenance, fabrication, equipment and HSEQ-adjacent businesses — which have volume, urgency and fewer internal shared-service resources.

Instant Expert

A five-minute brief so you can talk to a mining services & mets prospect like someone who understands their world — not a caller reading a script.

The labour market they live in

WA's resources economy keeps demand for mining-services labour high and wages higher. METS SMEs compete with the majors for both site crews and the office staff — schedulers, procurement officers, document controllers — who keep contracts compliant. FIFO patterns and remote sites make local administrative hiring even harder.

Why the admin load hurts

Supplying to mining means meeting big-company standards: HSEQ document control, mobilisation packs, pre-quals, timesheets, procurement follow-up, maintenance records, tender submissions and reporting. A services SME rarely has a shared-service team, so this lands on operations managers and coordinators — pulling them off delivery and putting contract compliance at risk.

The pressures shaping their year

Work comes in urgent, high-volume peaks tied to shutdowns, mobilisations and contract wins. Client audits and pre-qualification are unforgiving on documentation. Cost pressure from principals is constant, and every hour a coordinator spends chasing paperwork is an hour not spent winning or delivering the next job.

Where offshore support fits

Field capability and site decisions stay local. A dedicated offshore resource handles the structured back-office load — HSEQ registers, mobilisation admin, procurement follow-up, timesheet processing, maintenance-planning support and reporting — keeping documentation and responsiveness consistent through the peaks.

Talk like an insider

Know the language: mobilisation, pre-quals, HSEQ, JHA/JSA, SWMS, shutdowns, mob/demob, plant and equipment registers, POs and variations. Know the tools: Pronto, Pegasus/Avetta, CargoWise-adjacent systems, SharePoint document control. Speak to compliance and responsiveness, not cheap labour.

Objection handling — mining services & mets

How to handle any objection. Acknowledge it sincerely, reframe to the business issue, then ask one question that keeps the conversation open. Never argue and never oversell — the goal is a short meeting for the Commercial Manager, not winning the point on the call.
ObjectionResponse angle
“Our work is all on-site.”The site work stays with your crews. We support the office trail behind it — HSEQ documents, mobilisation packs, procurement follow-up, timesheets. Which of those is heaviest through a shutdown?
“Client audits are strict.”Exactly why consistent document control matters. A trained, dedicated resource keeps HSEQ registers and mob packs audit-ready — you keep ownership of the standards.
“Data and IP security.”A real concern in resources. Brainbox staff work under managed access, NDAs and clear policies inside your systems — happy to detail the controls.
“We ramp up and down fast.”That's the strength — a dedicated offshore resource gives you steady documentation capacity through the peaks without hiring and shedding locally.
“Procurement's too specific.”The sourcing decisions stay with you; the chasing, PO entry and delivery follow-up don't need to. That's where the time goes.
“Send me details.”Happy to — is HSEQ documentation, procurement or mobilisation your bigger pressure? I'll send the right example.
Know when to stop. A clear "no" frees time for better-fit accounts. Close the loop kindly, set a nurture date if there's a future trigger, and respect every opt-out immediately.
