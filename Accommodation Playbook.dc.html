Section 1 · Orientation

Purpose & how this playbook fits

This playbook governs how Brainbox operates its residential accommodation — both the units let to the short-stay market and those used for visiting clients and staff. It is a subsidiary of the Fleet & Facilities Playbook: the parent sets the department’s systems, rhythms and chain of accountability; this document holds the accommodation-specific detail those rhythms depend on.

Brainbox manages ten units in total — five at LaGrande (LGr) and five at Hensonville (Hv). Some carry paying guests booked through Booking.com, Airbnb and Agoda; some host visiting clients; and some house our own people. However different a stay feels, the same standard of cleanliness, safety and responsiveness applies to every unit.

Where the boundaries sitThe parent Fleet & Facilities Playbook owns the daily operating rhythm, the cleaning window, work control in Asana, and the duty statements for each team member. This playbook owns the commercial short-stay operation, the guest experience, and the unit-by-unit standards. Where the two touch — cleaning triggers, maintenance intake — this document follows the parent exactly.
The promise to every guestA clean, safe, well-presented unit; a fast, kind response to any issue; and a stay that earns a five-star review. Commercial performance follows guest experience — never the other way around.

Section 3 · How it runs

Operating model & systems of record

Two systems govern every decision. Discipline in how we use them is what keeps the operation visible, auditable and free of double-bookings. If a system is wrong, it is corrected before any work proceeds.

Uplisting

The channel manager and single source of truth for availability, rates, reservations and guest messaging. It syncs one calendar across Booking.com, Airbnb and Agoda so a night sold on one channel closes on all. Every booking, block and rate change lives here.

Asana

The work register for cleaning, repairs and maintenance — inherited from the parent playbook. If a task isn’t in Asana, it isn’t happening. Photos and descriptions attach to every item.

The distribution channels

ChannelRole
Booking.comHigh-volume OTA; strong for corporate and international guests.
AirbnbLeisure and longer-stay guests; review-driven, host-profile matters.
AgodaStrong regional and Asia-Pacific demand; complements the other two.
UplistingAggregator and control layer over all three — not a demand channel, but the system that keeps them consistent.
The golden rule of channelsNever edit a calendar or rate directly in Booking.com, Airbnb or Agoda. All changes are made in Uplisting, which pushes them out. This is how double-bookings are prevented.

Section 5 · Bookings

Bookings — the reservation lifecycle

Every reservation follows the same path, whether it is a paying guest or a visiting client. The steps below are managed end to end in Uplisting.

Step 1
Enquiry / booking

Received via a channel or a client request; captured in Uplisting.

Step 2
Confirm & hold

Dates blocked across all channels; client holds take priority.

Step 3
Pre-arrival

Welcome message, access details, house rules and arrival time.

Step 4
Check-in & stay

Access confirmed; in-stay messaging monitored by Jho.

Step 5
Check-out & review

Turnover triggered; a review is requested from the guest.

Booking rules

  • Client priority. Visiting-client needs are placed as holds before nights are released to the market.
  • Minimum stay & lead time. Set per unit and season in Uplisting; short same-day gaps are avoided where a turnover can’t be resourced.
  • Payment & ID. Taken through the channel; guest identity verified before check-in per platform rules.
  • Cancellations. Follow the published channel policy; client cancellations release the night back to the market immediately.
One calendar, always currentA booking is only real once it is in Uplisting. Verbal or email holds are entered immediately, or they do not exist.

Section 6 · Commercial

Rates, availability & channel management

The commercial goal is simple: high occupancy at the best sustainable rate, with zero double-bookings. Uplisting is the control point for all of it.

Pricing

Rates are reviewed against demand, local events and season. Weekend, peak and multi-night rates are set deliberately; last-minute gaps may be discounted to lift occupancy without undercutting the base rate.

Availability

Client holds first, then open nights released to all channels. Maintenance days are blocked in Uplisting so a unit is never sold while it is out of service.

Channel parity

Rates and minimum stays are kept consistent across Booking.com, Airbnb and Agoda through Uplisting, so no channel is disadvantaged and no night is oversold.

Listing quality

Photos, descriptions, amenities and house rules are kept accurate and identical across channels. A listing that matches reality is the single biggest driver of good reviews.

Best practice we hold toDynamic, demand-based pricing; a minimum-stay policy that protects turnover capacity; complete, honest listings; and instant calendar sync. These are what separate a profitable short-stay operation from a stressed one.

Section 8 · Cleaning

Cleaning & the turnover standard

Cleaning follows the parent playbook exactly: Uplisting flags the trigger, and cleaning runs in the protected daily window. This page sets the unit-level standard every turnover must meet.

Triggers & window (from the Fleet & Facilities Playbook)Clean on turnover (same-day check-out and check-in) and for long-stay guests every third day. All accommodation cleaning happens in the fixed 11:00am–1:40pm window, in parallel across units. No discretionary cleaning.

Bedrooms & living

  • Strip & remake with fresh linen
  • Dust, surfaces & mirrors
  • Floors vacuumed & mopped
  • Rubbish removed, bins relined

Bathroom

  • Toilet, shower, basin sanitised
  • Fresh towels to par
  • Consumables restocked
  • Floor cleaned & dried

Kitchen

  • Benches & sink cleaned
  • Appliances wiped, fridge cleared
  • Crockery clean & stowed
  • Tea/coffee & welcome items restocked

Final check

  • Presentation & staging
  • Lights, aircon, wifi working
  • Defects photographed & logged
  • Unit marked ready in Uplisting
Ready means readyA unit is only released for the next guest when the turnover checklist is complete and any defect is either fixed or logged and assessed as non-blocking. If a clean can’t be resourced in the window, the night is not sold.

Section 9 · Supplies

Linen, consumables & amenities

Turnovers only run smoothly when stock is ready. Par levels — the minimum on hand — are held for every unit so a clean never stalls for want of a towel or a toilet roll.

CategoryHeld to par & restocked each turnover
LinenTwo full sets per bed (one on, one ready) plus a laundry buffer; towels to guest count.
Bathroom consumablesToilet paper, soap, shampoo, hand wash — topped to par every clean.
Kitchen & welcomeTea, coffee, sugar, dishwashing items, bin liners; a simple welcome touch.
Cleaning stockChemicals and materials held centrally; low stock flagged to Jho before it runs out.

Linen cycle

The one-on/one-ready model keeps every unit turnaround-able the same day. Soiled linen is bagged, laundered and returned to the buffer.

Reordering

Consumables are checked against par during each clean; anything below par is reported so it is reordered before the next turnover.

Never shortRunning out mid-turnover costs a booking-ready unit. Par levels exist so that never happens — the fix for a shortage is to raise par, not to skip an item.

Section 10 · Repairs

Repairs — reactive fault handling

Things break; guests notice fast. The response is disciplined: report, log, triage against guest impact, fix or escalate — never quietly, always in the system.

Step 1
Reported to Jho

Guest, cleaner or inspection raises it — not fixed quietly.

Step 2
Logged in Asana

Description and photos attached; unit and severity noted.

Step 3
Triaged

May prioritises against guest readiness and safety.

Step 4
Fixed or escalated

Arvin resolves, or a contractor is engaged with approval.

SeverityResponse
Urgent (safety / no access / no water / no power)Actioned immediately; if a guest is in-situ, offer a fix window or move them to another unit.
Guest-affecting (appliance, aircon, wifi)Resolved same day;; block the unit in Uplisting before the next check-in if it can’t be ready.
Non-blocking (cosmetic, minor wear)Logged and scheduled into a maintenance day between bookings.
Guest in the unitRepairs with a guest in-situ are arranged with their agreement, at a time that suits them, with clear communication. A moved guest is always offered an equivalent or better unit.

Section 11 · Prevention & safety

Preventive maintenance & safety compliance

The cheapest repair is the one prevented. Scheduled checks keep units guest-ready and, above all, safe — a non-negotiable in every unit, occupied or not.

Preventive schedule

Between bookings and on the weekly residential day, units are checked for wear, leaks, appliance condition, aircon filters, pest signs and presentation. Small issues are fixed before they become guest-affecting.

Deep clean cycle

Beyond turnovers, each unit gets a periodic deep clean — behind appliances, soft furnishings, windows and grout — scheduled into quiet periods.

Safety compliance — every unit

  • Working smoke alarms, tested and dated.
  • Fire extinguisher / blanket where required, in date.
  • First-aid kit stocked; emergency numbers displayed.
  • Electrical, gas and water checked for hazards.
  • Secure locks and functioning access on every door.
Safety is never traded for a bookingA unit with a failed smoke alarm, a security fault or an electrical hazard is blocked in Uplisting until it is remedied — no exceptions, staff housing included.

Section 15 · The rules

Non-negotiable rules & the outcome we want

  1. Uplisting is the single source of truth. All calendars, rates and bookings are managed there — never directly in a channel.
  2. Asana governs all work. If a clean, repair or task isn’t logged, it isn’t happening.
  3. Everything through Jho. One point of intake; nothing fixed quietly or booked off-system.
  4. Client holds before market nights. Visiting-client needs are placed first, always.
  5. Ready means ready. A unit is released only when clean, checked and safe.
  6. Safety is never traded for a booking. A unit with a safety fault is blocked until fixed.
The outcome we wantFull, well-run units; clean, safe stays; fast, kind responses; and reviews that keep demand high. Run this way, accommodation is a calm, profitable part of Facilities that reflects well on Brainbox — and always, in every interaction, be kind.
