Instant Expert

A five-minute brief so you can talk to a strata & property prospect like someone who understands their world — not a caller reading a script.

The labour market they live in

Strata and property management are notoriously high-churn: managers and assistants burn out under portfolio loads and leave, taking knowledge with them. WA firms struggle to recruit and retain the administrators who keep portfolios moving, so vacancies pile pressure onto the managers who remain.

Why the admin load hurts

Every property and every scheme generates a stream of work: maintenance requests and contractor follow-up, owner and tenant communication, invoice coding, arrears follow-up, insurance and compliance records, inspection booking and meeting administration. When it backs up, owners and committees complain, maintenance stalls and managers spend their day firefighting rather than managing relationships.

The pressures shaping their year

Portfolios grow faster than headcount, fee pressure is constant, and service expectations from owners, tenants and committees are high. Trust-accounting and compliance obligations are strict. Every hour a manager spends coding invoices or chasing a contractor is an hour not spent retaining and winning management rights.

Where offshore support fits

Owner, committee and tenant relationships stay with the local manager. A dedicated offshore administrator handles the process work — maintenance coordination, contractor compliance, arrears follow-up, invoice coding, inspection booking and communications — improving responsiveness and reducing backlog.

Talk like an insider

Know the language: strata schemes, levies, arrears, by-laws, AGMs/EGMs, common property, trust accounting, management rights, ingoing/outgoing inspections, work orders. Know the tools: StrataMaster, PropertyMe, Property Tree, Rockend, Console. Speak to responsiveness and manager retention.

Objection handling — strata & property

How to handle any objection. Acknowledge it sincerely, reframe to the business issue, then ask one question that keeps the conversation open. Never argue and never oversell — the goal is a short meeting for the Commercial Manager, not winning the point on the call.
ObjectionResponse angle
“It's all relationship work.”The owner and committee relationships stay with your managers. We support the process behind them — maintenance, arrears, invoice coding, inspections. Which backs up most?
“Trust accounting is sensitive.”Understood. A trained resource works inside your system under managed access and clear controls; you keep ownership of trust and compliance decisions.
“Owners expect local contact.”They still get it — the manager stays their point of contact. Offshore support just clears the backlog so the manager is more responsive.
“We tried outsourcing.”Usually scoping and supervision. Our managed model trains the person in your software and covers leave — not a gig VA.
“High churn makes training hard.”That's the value — a stable, dedicated offshore resource reduces the churn pain and keeps portfolio knowledge in place.
“Send me info.”Happy to — is it maintenance, arrears or invoice coding that hurts most? I'll send the right example.
Know when to stop. A clear "no" frees time for better-fit accounts. Close the loop kindly, set a nurture date if there's a future trigger, and respect every opt-out immediately.
